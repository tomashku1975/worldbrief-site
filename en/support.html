FAQ

What is Pale Blue Dot?

A world map showing what is happening now — events, conflicts, trade and energy flows, climate, geology and population — with a short narrative for the region you are looking at.

Where does the data come from?

From public sources, including GDELT and news outlets, USGS, NASA, NOAA, Copernicus, the United Nations, World Bank/IMF, Natural Earth and OpenStreetMap. The full list of sources with licences is available in the app.

Who writes the narratives?

Narratives are AI-generated summaries of public articles; sources are listed under each one. They may contain errors — check the sources for anything important.

Are the floods, earthquakes and cyclones in the app warnings?

No. They are information, not warnings. Official warnings are issued only by national and regional authorities.

Do I need an account?

No — the map and narratives work without signing in. An account (Apple, Google or anonymous) is only needed to watch regions, keep settings in your account and receive notifications.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Delete account. Details: account deletion.
