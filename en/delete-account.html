How to delete your Pale Blue Dot account

Pale Blue Dot: World Brief app (iOS, Android) · developer: Tomasz Żuczek

Option 1: in the app (immediate)

  1. Open Pale Blue Dot and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and data are deleted immediately and you are signed out.

Option 2: by email

Email privacy@palebluedot.news with the subject “Delete my Pale Blue Dot account” from the email address linked to the account (the one used with Sign in with Apple or Google). We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm by reply. Anonymous accounts (without email) can only be deleted in the app, as they cannot be linked to an email message.

What is deleted

  • the account: ID, email address, name (if stored),
  • your list of watched regions and “last seen” markers,
  • app settings stored in your account and alert read status,
  • push tokens (notifications stop),
  • sign-in sessions (all devices are signed out).

None of this data is retained. It disappears from database backups within 30 days at the latest. Settings stored only on your phone are removed by uninstalling the app.

Public content (regional narratives, hazard data shown on the map) is not user data and is not linked to your account.
