Perspective

At the end of every map is the same dot.

In 1990, from 6 billion kilometres away, Voyager 1 turned around and photographed Earth. Carl Sagan called it a pale blue dot — everything we know, in a single pixel.Hence the name. Tap the dot in the app and see it for yourself.

Video: NASA/Goddard · NASA/JHU APL/Carnegie Institution of Washington. Photo: NASA/JPL-Caltech.
